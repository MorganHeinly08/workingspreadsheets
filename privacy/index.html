Privacy

The calculators on this site run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent to a server, logged, or stored anywhere. There is no account and no sign-up.

What happens to what you type

Nothing leaves your device. Every calculation on this site is JavaScript arithmetic performed locally in the page. Student initials, scores, dates, caseload numbers, renewal dates — none of it is transmitted. Close the tab and it is gone. You can confirm this by opening your browser's network tab while using any calculator, or by disconnecting from the internet and watching the calculators keep working.

Analytics

This site currently runs no analytics at all. No cookies are set, no profile is built, and no third-party script is loaded on any page. If that changes, this paragraph changes with it.

Links to Etsy

Links to workbooks go to our shop on Etsy. Once you follow one, you are on Etsy's site and Etsy's privacy policy applies — we do not control it. Those links carry a referral parameter that tells Etsy the visit came from this site, which reduces our selling fee. It identifies the site, not you.

YouTube

Our YouTube channel is operated through the YouTube Data API. That access lets us publish our own videos to our own channel. It does not read anything about viewers.

A few product pages embed the short walkthrough for that product. Those use YouTube's privacy-enhanced player on youtube-nocookie.com, which sets no advertising cookies unless you press play. Every other page on this site — including every calculator — contacts YouTube not at all.

Children

This site is aimed at working adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the bottom of this page changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify, because we do not have your address.

Contact

Questions are best sent through the shop, where they are attached to your order: SimpleDesigns on Etsy.
