Fundraiser Order Tracker

A fundraiser order tracker for school sales: type the order forms as they come back, log each payment, and get the vendor's size totals, a packing list for every class and a list of who still owes. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Orders: one row per item, with teacher, item and size dropdowns; price, totals and Paid, Part-paid or Unpaid status fill in
  • Size Totals: every item and size counted, ready to send the vendor or printer
  • Bag Lists: pick a teacher and print that class's packing list, with paid status and a box to tick
  • Unpaid: every order with money still owed, for reminder notes
  • Payments by method: collected, deposited and still to deposit, ready to hand the treasurer
  • Dashboard with orders, items sold, sales, collected, still owed, vendor cost, card fees, profit, goal progress, best items and top classes, plus a printable Order Form that builds itself
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
