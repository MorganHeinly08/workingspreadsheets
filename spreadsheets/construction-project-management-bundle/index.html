Construction Project Management Bundle

Eight connected construction project tools in one workbook: a submittal register, RFI log, change order log, punch list and 8-week lookahead, with a Summary of what needs attention across the job today. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Fill Setup once with the project, its dates and the project team, and every tool uses those lists
  • Submittal register with spec section, revision, reviewer and result; log the next revision of a Revise & resubmit and the old row shows Resubmitted, with ball in court on every row
  • RFI log with drawing or spec reference, who it went to, response due date, days open and ball in court
  • Change order log with cost and days impact, the owner's decision and days waiting; undecided changes older than 7 days show Overdue
  • Punch list where an item shows Verified only when someone other than the owner who fixed it signs it off
  • Task and milestone tracker, an 8-week lookahead bar chart that draws itself from Task IDs, a purchase order and delivery tracker, and a closeout checklist
  • 250 ready rows in every log; each Summary list shows the five most urgent items
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
