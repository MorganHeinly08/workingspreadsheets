Anecdotal Notes Tracker

An anecdotal notes tracker for teachers: every observation in one place, one row per note with what happened before and after, and a dashboard for each student. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Notes Log: pick the student and focus area, then record before, what you saw, count, minutes, people involved, after, your interpretation and any follow-up
  • A Check column turns green once the key fields are in, so thin notes stand out before a meeting
  • Dashboard for one student or All Students: total notes, last 30 days, times observed, average minutes, open follow-ups and last date observed
  • Amber flag when a student has not been observed in the number of days you choose, plus a focus-area mix and the 10 newest notes
  • Student roster of up to 100 with notes logged and last observed filled in automatically
  • Getting Started has five rules for objective notes with do and don't examples, and six focus areas you can rename
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
