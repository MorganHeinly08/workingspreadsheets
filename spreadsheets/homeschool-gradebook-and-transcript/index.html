Homeschool Gradebook and Transcript

A homeschool gradebook that turns your grades into a transcript: every child and every course in one file, with report cards, credits toward graduation and a log of school days and hours. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Up to 6 students in one file, with courses by student and year at Standard, Honors, AP, Dual enrollment or Pass/Fail levels
  • Grade Log: pick the course, type the score and points possible; M counts as missing, EX is excused
  • Report Card for any student and school year, with grade, letter, missing work, your comments, year GPA, credits, days and hours
  • Transcript by grade level with credits, letters, grade points, cumulative unweighted and weighted GPA, the grade scale and a signature line
  • Credit Tracker shows credits earned, in progress and still needed in each subject area, with a chart
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
