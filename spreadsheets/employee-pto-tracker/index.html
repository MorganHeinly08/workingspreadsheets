Employee PTO Tracker

An employee PTO tracker for Excel: vacation, sick days and half-days for up to 200 employees, with balances that reset on each work anniversary or on January 1, an automatic dashboard and a weekly who's-out view. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • PTO allowance, used and remaining for every employee; you choose which leave types deduct PTO
  • Each leave type resets on its own schedule: work anniversary or January 1
  • More vacation with length of service, and sick days by salary or hourly pay type
  • Dashboard with KPI cards, monthly trend, absence mix and top 5 absentees, plus weekly availability
  • Team Report and a one-employee annual report with a color-coded calendar
  • Works for any year, with full-day and half-day codes
  • Opens in Excel
