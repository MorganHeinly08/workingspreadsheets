Employee Timesheet & Overtime Calculator

An employee timesheet with an overtime calculator built in: log punches and breaks or just type the hours, and every pay period ends in a payroll-ready summary with regular, overtime and double time already split. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Time Entries: 3,000 rows for clock in, clock out and break or typed hours, with daily and weekly overtime worked out for you
  • Overtime rules: weekly, daily + weekly (California style, with double time) or none
  • Flag column catches missing punches, days over 16 hours and possible duplicates
  • Pay Period Summary for any period: regular, overtime, double time, PTO, holiday, sick and a gross pay estimate, with an Approved column
  • Printable one-page weekly time card with signature lines
  • Dashboard with hours and overtime this pay period, open flags and a 12-week overtime trend
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
