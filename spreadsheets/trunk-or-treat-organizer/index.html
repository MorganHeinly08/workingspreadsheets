Trunk-or-Treat Organizer

A complete trunk-or-treat planning spreadsheet for churches, schools and community groups. One live Event Dashboard rolls up your trunk sign-ups, confirmed arrivals, volunteers, candy estimate and budget automatically, so a single coordinator can run the whole event from the first sign-up to clean-up. Works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

  • Built-in candy calculator estimates how many pieces to buy from your expected crowd and pieces per child
