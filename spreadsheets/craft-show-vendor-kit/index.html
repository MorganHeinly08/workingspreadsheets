Craft Show Vendor Kit

A craft show vendor kit that answers the only question that matters after a market - was the booth fee worth it - by tracking sales, costs, stock and margins per show, then showing net profit and earnings per hour side by side across the season. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Every item you bring, flagged for restock
  • Booth value and units in stock, totalled as you type

  • Your sales logged by date, item and payment method, feeding every total automatically
  • Margin dollars and margin percent on every product, from your materials cost and price
  • Packing checklist for the booth
  • Net profit per show, after the booth fee and every other cost you enter
  • Break-even units before the doors open, and what you really earned per hour
