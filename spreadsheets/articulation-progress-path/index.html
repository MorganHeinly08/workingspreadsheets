Articulation Progress Path

A Microsoft 365 Excel articulation tracker that connects each student's speech-sound targets with a visual progress road, dated observation records and a printable student summary. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Progress Path: choose a student and target to see the current practice stage and completed milestones on a visual road
  • Customize up to six stages for each speech-sound target
  • Record dated observations with correct responses, opportunities, support and practice setting
  • Review selected-stage observations and an accuracy chart; optional numeric criteria flag evidence for your review
  • Caseload view keeps multiple students and targets organized
  • Entering observations never completes a stage for you: you define the stages, criteria and progression
  • Prepared entry space for 100 students, 300 targets, 1,800 stages and 3,000 observations, including the example records
  • Opens in Excel
