Christmas Gift Giving Tracker

A Christmas gift giving tracker for everyone on your list: who you are buying for, what you picked, what it cost and how much budget is left, with 2,400 gift ideas built in. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Up to 50 recipients with a live dashboard of budget allocation, spending, gift status and top recipients

  • Black Friday planner: one season budget, a gift list per person, a deals tab and a purchase log with a running total
