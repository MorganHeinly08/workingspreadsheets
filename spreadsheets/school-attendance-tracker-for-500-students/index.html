School Attendance Tracker for 500 Students

A school attendance tracker for up to 500 students with an absence log built in: tick who is here each day, log why the others were not, and attendance percentages, absence counts and per-student reports fill themselves in. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Up to 500 students and a full school year, one tick box per school day, grouped by week
  • JUMP TO lands on this week, and an ALL PRESENT row marks the whole day so you only tick the exceptions
  • Absence Entry: date, student, type (Sick, Excused, Unexcused, Tardy, Other), reason and note, with a NOT LOGGED YET list
  • Attendance Report and Absence Report per student, with monthly tables and the most recent absences
  • Holidays, breaks and days outside your dates are never counted, and percentages count only school days up to today
  • Opens in Excel
