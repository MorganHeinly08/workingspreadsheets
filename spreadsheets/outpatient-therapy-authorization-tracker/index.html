Outpatient Therapy Authorization Tracker

An authorization tracker for outpatient PT, OT and SLP clinics: see how many visits are left on every authorization, which plans of care need a signature or a recert, and which progress reports are due. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Plans of Care with certified-through, sent and signed dates, frequency and an optional referral expiry; a recert is a new row, so the history stays
  • Progress reports counted in treatment days from the start of the episode, the way Medicare counts them
  • Authorizations in visits or units with used, remaining, burn ratio, projected use, the weekly pace you need and a submit-re-auth-by date
  • Visit Log with timed minutes and untimed codes; units calculate from timed minutes and every visit is checked against its authorization
  • Payer rules you control: which payers need an authorization, a progress report every N treatment days and how long a certification can run
  • Dashboard: plans to act on, progress reports due, re-auths due, visits with no auth, unsigned notes and arrival rate, with a burn-down chart
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
