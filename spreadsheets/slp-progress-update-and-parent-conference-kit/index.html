SLP Progress Update and Parent Conference Kit

An SLP progress update and parent conference kit for Excel that turns target-level speech and language observations into a clear family update, a conference plan and a follow-up list. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Update Path: six milestones for each student and reporting period, from targets selected to follow-up reviewed, with the next action; it tracks your preparation, never student mastery
  • Caseload view of overdue updates, incomplete records and open or overdue follow-ups
  • Evidence: numeric or narrative observations with activity, measurement conditions and cue or support level; a 0 is a real score
  • Goal Updates: your descriptor, plain-language meaning, strengths and next steps
  • Conferences and Follow-Up: caregiver questions, discussion points and agreed actions with an owner and a due date
  • Printable Family Update, Conference Plan and Home Practice Plan, Letter portrait, one page wide
  • Observations are never averaged across targets, tasks or support levels; private working notes stay off the family pages
  • Opens in Excel
