Handwriting Support Reference Placemat

A pediatric OT cheat sheet for handwriting: pick a focus and one page shows what to look for, a visual example on a ruled line, cues to say, praise, paper and tool options and a desk sketch of the workspace. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • OT Placemat: choose one of six focuses written out in full: baseline alignment, word spacing, consistent letter size, letter starting points, copying from a model and page organization
  • Numbered desk sketch with five things to look at: chair and table height, paper position, helper hand, functional pencil hold and a clear view of the model
  • The focus's example words on a ruled line, the aim, the common mix-up and a paper suggestion
  • Helpful cues, two short scripts, praise wording and your own cue; the letter formation reference and paper guides highlight for the focus you pick
  • OT Setup tab: a group label, your cue, what helps and a next-time note for each focus; switching never erases them
  • OT Reference tab: every word the placemat shows is editable, to match your school's handwriting program
  • 6 printable placemats, one page per focus, in US Letter and 11x17 (tabloid)
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
