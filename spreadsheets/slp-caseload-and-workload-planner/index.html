SLP Caseload and Workload Planner

An SLP caseload and workload planner for school speech therapists: enter each student once and log each session once, and your schedule, service minutes, make-ups, dates and workload all update from that data. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Home: caseload, weekly planned minutes, minutes delivered, the minutes gap, make-ups, upcoming dates and direct vs indirect hours
  • Caseload Hub and Service Plan: frequency and minutes per session become planned weekly minutes, checked against your schedule
  • Schedule Builder with an overlap check and a generator that turns your repeating week into session rows, skipping non-school days
  • Service Minutes by month, school and provider, plus a Make-Up Tracker for missed sessions
  • Important Dates for IEP, reevaluation and progress dates with days remaining
  • Daily, Weekly, Month and Year Planners that fill themselves in from your log
  • Opens in Excel
