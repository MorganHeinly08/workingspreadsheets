Bead and Jewelry Supply Inventory Spreadsheet

A bead and findings inventory spreadsheet for jewelry makers: track your stash, see what needs reordering, and cost every piece you make from your own inventory. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Dashboard tiles: materials tracked, stock value, low or out of stock, open purchase orders, spend logged and designs costed
  • Master inventory with supplier, SKU, bin location, unit cost, quantity on hand and your reorder point
  • Product costing: pick a Material ID, enter how much one piece uses, and the material cost fills in
  • Purchase log from Ordered to Received, with a low-stock queue that builds itself
  • Pricing and margin from your labour rate, Etsy fees, payment processing and target margin
  • 25 sample rows marked SAMPLE so every formula works before you type
  • Opens in Excel
