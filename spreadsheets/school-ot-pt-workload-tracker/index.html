School OT / PT Workload Tracker

Caseload counts students. Workload counts hours — evaluation, documentation, meetings, equipment, travel — and the gap between the two is the whole argument you keep trying to have with your administrator. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • Seventeen OT and PT task categories, each flagged direct or indirect
  • Log every task, not only the ones with a student in the room
  • Hours by category charted horizontally — readable at a glance
  • Total hours against contracted hours, stated as a percentage
  • Equipment Log for splints, standers and adapted seating
  • Next check due calculated ninety days from last checked
  • One-page printable Administrator Summary with a written narrative
  • Contracted hours and weeks are the only two inputs the summary needs
