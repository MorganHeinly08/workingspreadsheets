First Aid Inventory Tracker

Track first aid supplies so kits stay stocked and nothing expires. Set up each item once with a par level and location, log every receipt and use, and the dashboard flags what to reorder, what is expiring soon, and what has already expired. Expiry Watch lists lots by date; an Inspection Log records unit checks (first aid kits, AEDs) with pass or fail. An Item Gallery gives a visual overview. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.
