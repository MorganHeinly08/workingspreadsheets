SLP Weekly Planner

An SLP weekly planner you fill in once a week: plan your therapy week in 15-minute blocks, see how booked each day is, and let every student's minutes total themselves. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • 15-minute rows from 8:00 AM to 3:45 PM, seven days across, on one screen
  • Type any date in the week box and the day names and dates update
  • A booked vs. free bar above every day and one percentage for the whole week
  • Minutes by student: type a code and it counts every slot that mentions that student, against their target
  • Filled example week plus a blank template tab to duplicate each week
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
