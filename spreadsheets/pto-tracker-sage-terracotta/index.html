PTO Tracker (Sage & Terracotta)

A PTO tracker for Google Sheets and Excel: add your team once, type a leave code on any day, and every vacation and sick balance, the dashboard and both reports update on their own. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Vacation and sick balances for every employee: allowance, used and left, in days and in hours
  • One PTO bank or separate vacation and sick balances; tracked-only leave such as unpaid days without touching a balance
  • Allowances that grow with service (0, 1, 3, 5 and 10+ years), a per-person override and different sick days for Salary and Hourly staff
  • Twelve color-coded month calendars with half-day codes, plus an Hours Log for partial days (up to 500 entries)
  • Dashboard with absences by month, mix by leave type, top 5 absentees and who's out in any week you pick
  • Team Report and a printable Employee Report; resets on January 1 or each work anniversary; 3 color themes
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
