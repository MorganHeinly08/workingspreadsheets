School Break Practice Plan and Return Check

A school break practice plan for Excel: choose what each student should practice before a break, give families a clear plan, and know what to check when students return. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Break Path: five steps for each student's break (focus skills selected, family plan prepared, plan shared, return checks recorded, review and next step recorded) and the next action
  • Caseload: every student's break cycle, open item, return-check window and next action
  • Students, Break Cycles and Skill Plans: family-friendly focus, measurement, units and planned support
  • Checks: dated before-break and return observations with protocol, support and context; a 0 is a real score
  • Family Plan: a printable home practice page for each family
  • Return Review: before and after side by side, with your interpretation and next step (printable)
  • Any break you name: you type the dates, there is no preloaded calendar; optional Family Feedback log and printable form
  • Opens in Excel
