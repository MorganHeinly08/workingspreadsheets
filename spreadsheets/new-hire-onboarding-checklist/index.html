New Hire Onboarding Checklist

A new hire onboarding checklist for Google Sheets or Excel: every pre-start, day-one and first-week task for each hire, with an owner and a due date set from their start date. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Summary each morning: who starts in the next 30 days, overdue onboarding tasks, documents still open, policy acknowledgments past due, 30-60-90 reviews and late training, with a readiness line for every hire
  • Standard onboarding steps set up once; add a hire, pick tasks, and phase, owner and due date fill in
  • 30-60-90 day plan per hire with expected outcomes, support needed, evidence and review dates
  • Document Receipt Register for required paperwork: requested, received, reviewer and where it is stored (status only, never copies)
  • Policy acknowledgments by version, buddy check-ins with open needs, and a Training Matrix of finished, open, late and unassigned modules
  • 250 hires on one Summary; start any time
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
