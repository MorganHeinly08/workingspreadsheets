IEP Meeting Prep Tracker for Case Managers

An IEP meeting prep tracker for case managers: see what is still missing before the next IEP meeting, who you are waiting on, and which follow-up actions are due. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Overview: meetings in the next 7 days, overdue preparation, follow-up due and an action queue
  • Meeting Map: six preparation areas for the meeting you pick, the next action, who is responsible and who you are waiting on
  • Meetings and Tasks: one record per meeting cycle, with owners, due dates, status and notes in one table
  • Checklist Setup: 18 editable preparation and follow-up tasks to copy for each new meeting
  • Meeting Snapshot: a printable one-page summary of the selected meeting (Letter)
  • Teacher Input, Family Input and Agenda & Actions as editable tabs, plus the same three pages as fillable PDF forms
  • Keep each annual cycle as its own record; reschedule, cancel or archive without losing history
  • Opens in Excel
