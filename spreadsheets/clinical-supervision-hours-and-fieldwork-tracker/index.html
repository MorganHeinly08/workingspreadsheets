Clinical Supervision Hours and Fieldwork Tracker

Projected completion date. The buyer's real question is 'at my current rate, when do I finish?' The file computes hours logged, hours remaining, current weekly average, and a projected completion date — then compares it to their deadline and flags 'ahead', 'on pace', or 'will miss by N weeks'. It also splits hours into the categories boards actually count separately: direct client, indirect, group supervision, individual supervision. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.
