Christmas Pageant Planner

A christmas pageant planner that keeps cast, rehearsals, costumes, the running order and volunteers on one set of tabs, with a dashboard showing how ready you actually are and how many days of prep are left. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Readiness dashboard: cast confirmed, roles still uncast, rehearsals set, costumes ready and days of prep left, with warning flags for what is behind
  • Cast list with roles, so an uncast part is visible weeks out rather than the week of
  • Rehearsal schedule with what is being run and who is needed
  • Costume and prop tracking through to ready, per role
  • Running order for the night itself, so the programme and the cues match
  • Volunteer list with roles and contacts - the people who make it happen but never appear on the cast list
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
