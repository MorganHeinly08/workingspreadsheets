Group Practice Clinician Productivity Dashboard

Per-clinician contribution margin. The owner's question is not 'how many sessions' but 'which clinicians are profitable after their split, and who is drifting below target'. The file computes billed, collected, clinician compensation at their split, and contribution per clinician per month, plus a caseload utilisation percentage against their contracted hours. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.
