Shift Coverage Planner

A shift coverage planner for small teams: build a two-week schedule and the grid flags anyone booked on approved time off, any shift short of your minimum headcount and anyone over their weekly hours, before you post it. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • 14-day schedule grid for up to 100 active employees, filled from a dropdown of your shift codes
  • Red for booked on approved time off, orange for an unavailable weekday, yellow for two shifts in one day
  • Coverage tab: scheduled vs required vs gap for every shift on every day
  • Hours and Cost: daily, weekly and two-week hours per person, over-cap hours and labor cost
  • Printable Daily Roster for any date
  • Dashboard with KPI tiles, a coverage heat strip and gaps by weekday
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
