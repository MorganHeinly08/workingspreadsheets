Performance Review Comment Bank

A performance review comment bank that starts from your ratings: rate eight competencies for the team on one grid and the Review Studio writes the review for each person. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Ratings Grid: the whole team on eight competencies, with a weighted score and band
  • Review Studio writes a competency-specific sentence for each rating, top two strengths and two development areas
  • Phrase Bank of 200+ sentences across eight competencies and every narrative section
  • Names and pronouns correct throughout, for he, she and they
  • Team Calibration: band distribution plus leniency and straight-lining flags
  • Review Log of what was delivered and when; opens with sample data
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
