OT Handwriting Kit

An OT handwriting kit: a handwriting cheat sheet placemat that tells you what to look for and what to try, plus the adapted handwriting paper to print for it. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Choose a focus on the placemat: baseline alignment, word spacing, consistent letter size, letter starting points, copying from a model or page organization
  • One page shows a numbered desk sketch, a visual example on a ruled line, cues to say, praise wording, paper and tool options and a letter formation reference
  • Setup tab: type a group label, your cue, what helps and a next-time note for each focus; they show whenever that focus is picked
  • Reference tab: every word the placemat shows is editable, to match your school's handwriting program
  • Adapted Handwriting Paper in 10 styles, from three-line, highlighted middle and bold baseline to letter boxes and skip-a-line, each in 1 in, 3/4 in and 1/2 in writing sizes
  • Color and Low Ink versions in US Letter and A4; page 1 of each PDF says which paper fits which focus
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
