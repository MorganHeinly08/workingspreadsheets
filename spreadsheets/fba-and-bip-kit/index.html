FBA and BIP Kit

A functional behavior assessment kit for school teams: 18 working forms that take you from the referral to the BIP review, plus a workbook that graphs your ABC data and drafts the team's hypothesis statement. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • 18 forms in School and Clinic versions, each a printable PDF and an editable Word file: interviews, ABC observation, scatterplot, interval recording, FBA summary, BIP, fidelity checklist and BIP review
  • ABC Log: one row per incident with an optional setting event column, room for 500 incidents
  • Scatterplot of incidents by time block and weekday, shaded by count, with the busiest time written out
  • Graphs: eight graphs that redraw themselves, with a behavior picker
  • Summary: what was recorded most often, plus a hypothesis composer; the team picks the setting event, antecedent, behavior, consequence and function, and the sentence is written for you to edit
  • Replacement and Strategies: a menu of 40 strategies (prevent, teach, respond), each marked by whether it fits the team's hypothesized function
  • BIP Data with a phase-change line, a Fidelity Log (% of plan steps carried out as written) and a Review decision log
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
