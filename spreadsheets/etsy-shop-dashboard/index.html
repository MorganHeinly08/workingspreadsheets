Etsy Shop Dashboard

An Excel dashboard that reads the Order Items CSV you download from Etsy and turns it into a one-page view of your shop, compared with the period before. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Paste Etsy's Order Items CSV; the dashboard fills itself (up to 5,000 item rows)
  • KPI cards for net revenue, orders, average order value and repeat buyers vs the prior period
  • Revenue by product line, grouped by simple name rules or by Listing ID; nothing drops out of the totals
  • Top 10 listings, coupons used and where orders ship
  • 3, 6 or 12 month window and an optional monthly goal
  • Buyer names and addresses stay in your file; the Dashboard shows totals only
  • Opens in Excel
