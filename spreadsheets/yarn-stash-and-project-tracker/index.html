Yarn Stash and Project Tracker

A yarn stash tracker that tells a knitter or crocheter READY or SHORT before they cast on, and shows the whole stash as a wall of color with yardage, dye lots, value and how many years it would take to knit through at their current pace. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Dye lots tracked per skein, so the mismatch is not discovered halfway up a sweater
  • Your full stash inventory: dye lot, yardage left, storage location, and what it cost
  • Cost per wear on finished projects, from what the skeins actually cost
