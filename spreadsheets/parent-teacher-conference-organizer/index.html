Parent-Teacher Conference Organizer

A parent-teacher conference organizer for conference week: schedule every family, print a one-page prep sheet for each student, and track what you agreed to after each meeting. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Time slots build themselves from your conference days, start and end times, conference length, gaps and breaks
  • Schedule Builder flags a family booked twice, a time inside a break, siblings that are not back-to-back and times outside the family's preference, and marks interpreter conferences
  • Printable Day Schedule with family contact, language and sibling notes
  • One-page Prep Sheet per student with levels, attendance, strengths, areas for growth, goals, work samples and questions
  • Conference Log and Follow-Ups: agreed actions with an owner, due date and status, sorted by due date with overdue flags
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
