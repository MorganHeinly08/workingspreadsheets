SLP Cue-Level Data Sheet

A speech therapy data sheet for school SLPs: record the least cue for each target, and it calculates % independent and % accurate at any cue level for you. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • One code per target per probe session (I, MIN, MOD, MAX, ERR or NT) for up to 10 targets across 8 sessions
  • Two separate results for every target and session: % independent accurate and % accurate at any cue level; cued-correct is not an error
  • Blank cells are never counted, no-opportunity codes stay out of every denominator, and a dash shows until there is something to count
  • Mastery criterion (% independent across N sessions in a row) with run tracking, plus first-to-latest change
  • Settings: edit your cue key (up to 8 codes) and counting rules; dropdowns, the printed key and every calculation follow, with a CHECK column for setup mistakes
  • Filled, fictional /s/ articulation example across 8 weekly sessions
  • The data sheet prints on one landscape US Letter page
  • Opens in Excel
