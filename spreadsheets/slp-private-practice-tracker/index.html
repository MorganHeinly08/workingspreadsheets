SLP Private Practice Tracker

A speech therapy caseload system for a private practice — ten clients, six goals each, automatic progress maths, authorization renewals that count themselves down, and session minutes totalled ready for superbills. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • Five tiles across the whole practice, plus a per-client filter
  • Every goal ranked with a progress bar and a mastery badge
  • A stale-data flag on anyone not seen in fourteen days
  • Caseload Manager: ten clients, entered once, wired to their tabs
  • S01-S10: six goals each with counts, trials and automatic percentages
  • Service Log: session minutes totalled by month for superbills
  • Authorization renewal and plan review countdowns
  • Build to 100% or Reduce to 0% — both goal directions handled
