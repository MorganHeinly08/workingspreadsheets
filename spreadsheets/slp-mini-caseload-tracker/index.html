SLP Mini Caseload Tracker

A speech therapy data system sized for a real school caseload — ten students, six goals each, percentages and progress bars that calculate themselves, and one dashboard that shows who is progressing and whose data has gone stale. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • Five tiles across every student, plus a per-student filter
  • Every goal ranked with a progress bar and a mastery badge
  • A stale-data flag on anyone not seen in fourteen days
  • Caseload Manager: ten students, entered once, wired to their tabs
  • S01-S10: six goals each with counts, trials and automatic percentages
  • Service Log: session minutes totalled by month
  • Editable goal type bank feeding every dropdown
  • Build to 100% or Reduce to 0% — both goal directions handled
