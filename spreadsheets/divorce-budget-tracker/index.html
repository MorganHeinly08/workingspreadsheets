Divorce Budget Tracker

A divorce budget spreadsheet that turns one shared household budget into two separate forecasts, so you can see what your life will actually cost after a separation. It funds the legal process alongside the move-out and setup costs of the first few months, then projects your cash month by month. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Start from your current joint budget, marking what continues, reduces or stops after separation
  • Household Snapshot compares today against your household and theirs, line by line, with the difference shown
  • Forecast each household separately, so both sides can see whether the numbers actually work
  • One-time move-out and setup costs budgeted alongside the ongoing bills, which is where most plans come up short
  • Legal and professional fees tracked as part of the same picture rather than as a surprise
