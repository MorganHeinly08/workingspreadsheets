Missed Session & Makeup Tracker

A small, fast file that answers one question properly: who are you still owed sessions by, how long have they been waiting, and why were they missed in the first place. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • One row per missed session with an editable reason code
  • Eight reasons ready to use, from student absent to early release
  • Makeup required, makeup date and makeup completed per row
  • Outstanding makeups counted per student
  • Days since the oldest outstanding makeup
  • Students with three or more outstanding surfaced
  • Reason breakdown so patterns show up
  • Deliberately small — one job, done cleanly
