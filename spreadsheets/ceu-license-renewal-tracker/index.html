CEU & License Renewal Tracker

Four credentials on four different clocks, tracked together — what each cycle requires, what you have actually earned inside its window, and how many days you have left on each. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • One row per credential with its own cycle length and requirement
  • Days remaining and status calculated per credential
  • Only hours dated inside a cycle window count toward it
  • Credits earned against credits required, per cycle
  • Expiring within 30 days in red, within 90 in amber
  • A rolling 24-month calendar of every renewal date
  • Course cost tracked, so the annual CE spend is visible
  • Certificate-filed flag on every course
