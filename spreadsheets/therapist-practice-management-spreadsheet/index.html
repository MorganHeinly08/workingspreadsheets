Therapist Practice Management Spreadsheet

A practice management spreadsheet for private practice: log each session once and fees, no-show charges, client balances and superbills fill in on their own. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.
