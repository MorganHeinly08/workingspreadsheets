Benefits Open Enrollment Tracker

An open enrollment tracker for small businesses: see who has turned in their benefits forms, who still owes them, and what every plan costs per paycheck, in one spreadsheet you can hand straight to payroll. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Status Board: days left in the window, percent complete, who still owes forms and a reminder email list ready to paste into BCC
  • Elections log per employee per benefit, with a Check column that flags a wrong tier or a missing waiver reason
  • Rate Sheet: what each plan and tier costs the employee per month and per paycheck, with the change from last year
  • Deductions: the per-paycheck deduction schedule for payroll, every employee by benefit
  • Cost Summary: employer and employee cost per month and per year, by plan and by benefit type
  • Up to 15 plans and 150 employees, plus a filled example for a fictional 30-person company
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
