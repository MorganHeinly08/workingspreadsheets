Speech Therapy Group Schedule Builder

The week every school SLP dreads, done in one file: a Monday-to-Friday grid in 15-minute blocks that fills itself from your groups, checks every student's minutes, and tells you where you have double-booked someone. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • A Monday-to-Friday grid in 15-minute blocks, 7:30am to 3:30pm
  • Groups fill the grid automatically from day and start time
  • Every student checked against their required weekly minutes
  • Under-minutes students flagged before the schedule is final
  • Double-booking check across every time block
  • Bell schedules and specials held per building for reference
  • Roster carries teacher, building and do-not-schedule notes
  • No macros — it opens on a district Chromebook in Google Sheets
