IEP Caseload Checklist

An IEP checklist for case managers: tick off 10 steps per student, from Parent Contact to Final Copy Sent, and completion, days left, timeline and next step calculate for the whole caseload. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.
