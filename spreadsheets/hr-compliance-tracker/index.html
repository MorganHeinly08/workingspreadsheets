HR Compliance Tracker

An HR compliance tracker for small teams: type each hire date and the date each certification, license or document was issued, and the workbook works out every expiry, probation end, new hire review and annual review, then lists what is due next. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Certifications and Documents: issue date in, expiry date and status out (Current, Due soon, Expired, Missing, No expiry)
  • Probation, 30/60/90-day and annual review dates calculate from each hire date
  • Upcoming: everything due in the next 30, 60, 90 or 180 days, soonest first, with the action to take
  • This Month calendar of every key date, birthdays and work anniversaries included
  • Training Log with hours, trainer and renewal date
