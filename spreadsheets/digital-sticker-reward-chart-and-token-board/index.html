Digital Sticker Reward Chart and Token Board

A sticker reward chart and token board you fill in on screen: tick a box or type a point and a sticker appears straight away, boards fill up, and the reward unlocks at the goal. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Eight token boards of 5, 10 or 20 tokens, each with the behavior, the reward and an Earned count; a full board turns gold with GOAL, reward unlocked
  • Six text-only First / Then boards with optional 3 or 5 tokens to earn first, no picture icons needed
  • Four weekly sticker charts with up to five behaviors by day, daily and weekly totals and a weekly goal
  • Class Points for up to 30 students in teams, with a gold star for the top scorer, a class goal meter and six team meters
  • Print any token board or sticker chart big on one landscape page, with unearned spots left empty for paper stickers
  • 12 built-in sticker symbols in 8 colors, plus a Settings tab for stickers, rewards, behaviors and team names
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
