Private Practice Superbill & Claim Tracker

The billing side of a solo therapy practice, tracked properly: what you billed, what actually landed, which claims are aging past 90 days, and which payer is quietly denying more than the rest. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • Billed, collected and outstanding, calculated from the session log
  • Average days to payment, so you know who is slow
  • Denial rate overall and per payer
  • Claim aging bucketed into current, 31-60, 61-90 and 90 plus
  • Anything past 90 days flagged in red
  • Payer summary — submitted, paid, denied, average days, per payer
  • A one-page client statement that pulls from the session log
  • Rates held per client and applied to sessions automatically
