Classroom Economy and Behavior Tracker (Grades 3-5)

A classroom economy and behavior tracker for grades 3-5: score each block, and Class Bucks balances, a daily behavior report home, student graphs and a weekly class summary fill themselves in. The files open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Daily Tracker: one row per student per day (3,000 rows); pick a 0-3 score for each block and points, % earned and goal met calculate on their own
  • Student Dashboard: pick a student to see days logged, average % earned, last-5-day average, toughest and strongest block, a 'what to do next' tip and three graphs
  • Class Summary: pick a week to see every student's status (On track, Watch, Needs support), who to check in with first and a status chart
  • Rewards: class reward progress bar, every student's Class Bucks balance and a 500-row ledger for bonuses and spending from a reward menu you edit
  • 6 printables (Home Note, Point Card, Contract, Reflection, Self-Check and Token Board) that fill in from your data for a student and date and print on one page
  • Roster of up to 60 students; blocks run from Morning Meeting to Dismissal and you can rename or turn off any of them
  • Start any time of year; demo data is included and Start Here lists the ranges to clear
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
