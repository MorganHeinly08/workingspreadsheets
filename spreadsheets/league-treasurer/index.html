League Treasurer

A treasurer's ledger for any group that pools money - bowling and golf leagues, poker nights, rec teams, card clubs and fishing clubs. Set your buy-in and payout split once, then log dues, scores, attendance and fines as the season runs. The dashboard shows what has been collected, what is still outstanding and who owes it, and the standings and payouts work themselves out. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Live treasurer dashboard showing total pot, collected, outstanding, members paid and attendance rate
  • Dues tracker with payment date and method - status flips to Paid, Partial or Owed on its own
  • Payout split you control, with a total that flags itself if it does not add up to 100%
  • Rename the period from Week to Night, Session or Round and every header in the file follows
  • Attendance grid that counts each member's turnout and works out their percentage
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
