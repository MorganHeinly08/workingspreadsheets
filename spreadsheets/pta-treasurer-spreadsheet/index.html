PTA Treasurer Spreadsheet

A PTA treasurer spreadsheet for PTOs and booster clubs: enter every transaction once on the register and the budget, fundraiser tracker, monthly treasurer report and year-end audit packet build themselves. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • 500-row transaction register with running balance, categories, events, check numbers and cleared status
  • Budget against actual with ON BUDGET, WATCH and OVER BUDGET flags
  • Dues roster for 200 families: PAID, PARTIAL, PAID AHEAD and OWED
  • Fundraisers ranked by net against goal, plus reimbursements from Submitted to Paid
  • Monthly treasurer report you pick and print, and a bank reconciliation that turns green when it agrees
  • Year-end summary, 12-item audit and handoff checklist, and printable deposit and reimbursement forms
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
