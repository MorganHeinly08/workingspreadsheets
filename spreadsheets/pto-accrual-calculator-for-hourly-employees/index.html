PTO Accrual Calculator for Hourly Employees

A PTO calculator for hourly employees: enter the hours each person worked every pay period and their paid time off accrues at your rate, stops at your balance cap and rolls into next year by your carryover rule. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What you get

  • Accrue per hour worked or a fixed number of hours each pay period, with up to five tenure tiers
  • Waiting period, balance cap, carryover cap and payout rule applied at each pay period end
  • Hours Worked and PTO Used: one column per pay period, up to 53 a year
  • Balances: every employee's balance after each pay period, plus hours lost to the cap
  • Printable one-page PTO balance statement for any employee as of any pay period
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
