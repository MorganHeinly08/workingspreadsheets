School Counselor Follow-Up Tracker

A counselor follow-up tracker: eleven steps per case, from Referral to Documentation, with three follow-ups, a Resolution step, referral category and new referrals in the last 30 days. It opens in Microsoft Excel, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • 11 steps per case as checkboxes, including Follow-Ups 1 to 3 and Resolution
  • Referral Category per case, with cases by category and by grade on the dashboard

  • Dashboard: open cases, overdue, due soon, resolved and new referrals
