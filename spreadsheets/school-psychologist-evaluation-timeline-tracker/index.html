School Psychologist Evaluation Timeline Tracker

Every evaluation from referral to report to meeting, on one page, with the days remaining on each one calculated from the consent date you entered — and a protocol inventory so you never open an empty kit the week testing is due. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is reusable every school year.

What it does

  • Every evaluation tracked from referral through report to meeting
  • Days remaining calculated from the consent date you enter
  • Ten evaluation stages and four statutory intervals, all editable
  • The fifteen nearest deadlines surfaced on the dashboard automatically
  • Overdue cases flagged in red before they become a problem
  • Assessment Inventory tracks protocol supply and flags reorders
  • Twelve-month view clusters due dates so you can see the crunch coming
  • No test scores or results are stored anywhere in the file, by design
