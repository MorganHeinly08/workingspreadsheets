MTSS and RTI Small Group Scheduler

An MTSS and RTI small group scheduler: place students into tiered intervention blocks and check every student's minutes without anyone landing in two places at once. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Roster with intervention area, tier and required minutes for each student
  • Intervention groups of up to six by day, start time and duration
  • Weekly grid fills itself, color-coded by intervention area
  • Checks: every student against required minutes (Met, UNDER or NOT SCHEDULED) plus double-booking flags
  • Every tier, area and dropdown editable on the Lists tab
  • Also in the download: Reading Intervention Tracker, WCPM Progress Monitoring, Reading and Math Intervention Schedule Builders
  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
