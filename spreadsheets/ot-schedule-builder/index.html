OT Schedule Builder

An OT schedule builder for school-based occupational therapists: enter your caseload and groups once, and the weekly grid fills itself and checks every student's required minutes. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Caseload entered once, with each student's required OT minutes per week
  • Group sessions of up to six students by day, start time and duration
  • Weekly grid fills itself, color-coded by service type and building
  • Every student checked against required minutes: Met, UNDER or NOT SCHEDULED
  • Double-booking detection for a student placed in two sessions at once

  • Opens in Excel and in Google Sheets
