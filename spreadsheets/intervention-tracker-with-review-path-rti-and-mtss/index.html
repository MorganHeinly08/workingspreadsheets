Intervention Tracker with Review Path (RTI and MTSS)

An RTI and MTSS intervention tracker for Excel: bring each student's intervention plan, delivered sessions and progress scores together, then print a one-page Review Summary before the review meeting. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Review Path: six checkpoints for the selected student and cycle (Concern, Baseline, Plan, Delivery, Review, Next decision); finished paperwork is never confused with growth
  • Plans & Cycles: intervention, provider, schedule, one primary measure, baseline, goal and review date
  • Delivery Log: planned sessions, outcomes and minutes, with absences, closures, cancellations and reschedules counted separately
  • Progress Data: dated scores with conditions and an include or exclude choice, plus a baseline-to-latest and goal comparison
  • Review Summary: a printable page with a progress chart (Letter, portrait, one page wide)
  • Room for 100 students, 300 intervention cycles, 3,000 delivery rows and 3,000 progress rows
  • Not grade specific: you enter your own reading or math skill, measure, baseline and goal
  • Opens in Excel
