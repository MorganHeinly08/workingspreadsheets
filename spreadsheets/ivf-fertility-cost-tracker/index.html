IVF & Fertility Cost Tracker

Fertility treatment is billed in fragments: the clinic, the pharmacy, the lab, the anaesthetist, each on its own schedule. The total is genuinely hard to know while you are in it, and that is exactly when people need it. It opens in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets, has no macros and nothing to install, and is yours to reuse as often as you like.

  • Dedicated medication log for stim meds (Gonal-F, Menopur, trigger, progesterone) with pharmacy price comparison
  • Insurance claim tracker with status (submitted, approved, denied, appealed, reimbursed) so nothing slips through
  • Dashboard with total out-of-pocket, insurance covered, % covered, budget remaining, and lifetime-benefit remaining
