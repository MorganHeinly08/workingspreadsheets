IEP Progress Report Organizer and Goal Evidence Tracker

An IEP progress report organizer for Excel: connect each student's goals, dated evidence and reporting-period updates, see what still needs attention before progress reports go home, and print a one-page Student Snapshot. The files open in Microsoft Excel, have no macros and nothing to install, and are yours to reuse as often as you like.

What it does

  • Report Path: six checkpoints for the selected student and period (select goals, gather evidence, draft updates, review, share, file) and the next action
  • Caseload: who is due, overdue, missing evidence, drafted, shared and filed
  • Evidence: numeric or narrative observations with conditions and an include choice for the report
  • Goal Updates: your progress descriptor, narrative and next steps; Report Register holds due dates and checkpoint dates for every report
  • Student Snapshot: a printable page built from the evidence you choose to include (Letter, portrait, one page wide)
  • Room for 100 students, 12 reporting periods, 500 goals, 3,000 evidence entries, 2,000 goal updates and 1,000 report records
  • Start any time of year; Start Here instructions, record checks and fictional sample records included
  • Opens in Excel
